LibreLinkUp setup

This is not the account of the FreeStyle Libre app. To start following:

  1. Open the FreeStyle Libre app and choose Share or Connected Apps in the menu.
  2. Turn on the LibreLinkUp connection and invite a separate e-mail address.
  3. Install LibreLinkUp on any phone, create the account for that address and accept the invitation.
  4. Enter that account here. Use it only for this app: several apps on one account cause "too many requests" errors.
